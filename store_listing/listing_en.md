## Short description (max 80 characters)

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Harcama Takip is the easiest way to keep your daily spending, credit card statements and installments in order. Stop wondering where your money went at the end of the month; see everything on one screen.

★ Log expenses in seconds
Enter the amount, pick a category and a payment method, and you're done. Groceries, bills, transport, rent, subscriptions and many more categories are ready to use. Separate cash, debit card, credit card, bank transfer and automatic payments.

★ Credit card statements
Add your cards with their cut-off day and every expense lands in the right statement period automatically. See upcoming cut-off and payment due dates on the home screen.

★ Installment tracking
Enter an installment purchase once and every installment is placed in its month. Always know your remaining installment debt and next month's load.

★ Budgets and limits
Set a monthly budget and per-category limits. See at a glance how much of your budget is used and which category is over its limit.

★ Clear reports
Compare the last six months and explore your category breakdown to understand your spending habits.

★ Shared expenses with family and housemates
Create a team and manage shared expenses and budgets with the people you invite. Your personal expenses stay private.

★ Reminders
Get a reminder at the time you choose so you never forget to log the day's expenses.

★ Your privacy matters
• It does not connect to your bank and does not send money or make payments; it only stores the records you enter.
• It never asks for your full card number, expiry date or CVV; only the last 4 digits are kept to tell your cards apart.
• No ads, and your data is not shared with third parties.
• Delete your account and all your data from within the app at any time.

Available in Turkish and English, with light and dark themes.
